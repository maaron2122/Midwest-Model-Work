HOW IT
WORKS.

A few things to know while this storefront is getting ready.

Can I buy directly from this website?+

Not yet. This is a preview storefront and does not process payments. For current products and purchasing information, contact Midwest Model Works through Facebook.

Are the models shown available?+

The six current listings are visual examples only. They don't represent verified inventory, prices, or stock status.

How do I ask about a model?+

Add any models to your inquiry list, copy the message, and open the official Facebook page to send it. You can also contact the business there directly.

Will the site have online ordering?+

Online ordering can be added later once real inventory, payment processing, shipping options, and store policies are set up.
